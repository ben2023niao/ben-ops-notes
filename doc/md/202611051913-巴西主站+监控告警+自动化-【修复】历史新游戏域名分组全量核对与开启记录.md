| PP | 35 | 35 | 0 | 6/6 PASS |
| vendor_a | 123 | 123 | 0 | 6/6 PASS |
| G759 | 34 | 34 | 0 | 6/6 PASS |

1. 在 `configs.open_game_list` 中；
