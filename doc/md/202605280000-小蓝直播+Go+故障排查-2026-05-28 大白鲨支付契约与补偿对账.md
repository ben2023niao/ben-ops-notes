## 变更摘要

- `docs/api/openapi.yaml`、`packages/contracts/openapi/v1.yaml`：移除旧直签回调契约，新增 `/api/v1/hooks/payment/shark` 与 `/api/v1/hooks/payment/shark/refund`，补大白鲨回调 schema 和 `success` 文本响应。
- `apps/web/app/pages/recharge/index.vue`：充值页从 `/wallet/channels`、`/wallet/recharge/pay-types`、`/wallet/recharge/breakdown` 读取通道、可用方式和费用拆解。
- `apps/web/app/pages/recharge/qr.vue`：修复入口参数从 `pay_type` 读取，兼容旧 `channel`。
- `services/go-app/internal/repository/wallet/orders.go`：充值状态 IN 查询统一转 `[]int`，避免 `[]uint8` 被 GORM 当作 bytes；补待对账订单查询。
- `services/go-app/internal/service/wallet/reconcile.go`、`services/go-app/internal/worker/payment_reconcile.go`：新增大白鲨 QueryOrder 补偿对账，进程内定时扫描并补偿入账。
